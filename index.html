FAQ

Good questions to ask first.

Anything else? Send us a note.

Who is Northbank a good fit for?

Founders in roughly their first two years, usually with 2–25 people, who need finance, hiring or operations set up properly but aren't ready for a full-time hire to own it.

How is pricing worked out?

After the intro call you get a one-page written scope with deliverables, dates and a single fixed fee. If the scope doesn't change, the price doesn't either.

Do you replace our accountant?

No. We set up the workflow and reporting around your accountant or bookkeeper so their work is easier and your numbers arrive on time.

What happens after delivery?

Everything is documented and handed over to your team. Many founders book a short check-in a quarter later to tune things as they grow.
